## Authentication and authorization rules
- Login by email or Nigerian phone number (stored as `0XXXXXXXXXX`; `+234…` accepted). Passwords hashed with bcrypt; minimum 8 characters, mixed case and a number.
- 5 failed attempts per login and IP lock that pair for 60 seconds. Sign-up, reset and token endpoints are rate limited.
- Disabled accounts cannot log in. Active sessions and API tokens of a disabled account stop working on the next request.
- Session regenerated on login and invalidated on logout. Password change signs out other devices and revokes API tokens. Password reset revokes API tokens.
- Forgot-password gives the same response whether or not the email exists.
- Public sign-ups are always **Subscriber**. `user_type` and `status` are never mass assignable; only server code or an admin (Phase 3) changes them.
- Customer tier = `users.user_type` (one per user, drives pricing later). Only staff with `customers.change-type` can change it.

## Customer Users management (admin)
- Actions in `App\Actions\Customers`, each re-checking its permission: `ChangeUserType` (type, the only type path), `ChangeCustomerStatus` (the only status path), `UpdateCustomerProfile` (name, email, phone only; a changed email becomes unverified), `SendCustomerPasswordReset` (standard reset email; refused for disabled accounts).
- Disabling a customer revokes all their Sanctum API tokens and ends "remember me" logins; open web sessions are signed out by `EnsureUserIsActive` on the next request; login and new tokens are refused. Re-enabling does not restore old tokens.
- Details page shows safe fields only (name, email and verification, phone, type, status, joined, last login, number of API tokens). Passwords, hashes, remember/API tokens and last-login IP are never shown.
- Customers are never deleted (no route, no `customers.delete` permission); disable instead, so future wallet, transaction and referral records stay intact.
- No audit log yet (Phase 16); changes are reflected only in timestamps.
- Email verification: `NADABO_REQUIRE_EMAIL_VERIFICATION` (default false). Off: no emails, nobody blocked. On: verification email on sign-up and email change; unverified customers are sent to `/verify-email` (profile stays reachable).
- API tokens: lifetime from `SANCTUM_TOKEN_EXPIRATION` in minutes (empty/0 = no expiry); `expires_at` returned with each token; expired tokens pruned daily by the scheduler.

## Staff (System Users)
- Separate `system_users` table, `SystemUser` model, `admin` session guard. Customer accounts can never sign in at `/admin/login`; staff accounts can never sign in at `/login`.
- Separate session: cookie `nadabo_admin_session` limited to `/admin`, stored in `admin_sessions`. Signing out of one area does not affect the other.
- Separate login throttling (5 failures per email + IP).
- Disabled staff, or staff without a role, cannot sign in; a disabled account is signed out on its next request.
- Roles and permissions (spatie, `admin` guard). Super Admin passes every check while active.

## Approved decisions (Phase 11)
1. NIN, BVN and Exam PIN are separate customer-facing services, sold through the same `PurchaseService` as Data and Airtime. NIN and BVN purchases are digital service purchases, not KYC: the number is the one the service was bought for, never the customer's verified identity, and it is never copied into a customer or KYC record.
2. A NIN purchase takes the 11-digit NIN only and a BVN purchase the 11-digit BVN only, with the customer's consent in the approved sentence ("I confirm that the NIN I entered is correct and I consent to its submission for this service.", and the same for BVN; `config/purchases.php`) and fixed-price plans only.
3. NIN/BVN numbers are stored only encrypted. After confirmation, customers and staff see only the mask (seven dots and the last four digits); staff never see the full value. Staff find NIN/BVN purchases only through the dedicated exact-match POST search, and the ordinary GET search refuses NIN/BVN-shaped terms. NIN/BVN numbers are never put in URLs, logs, sessions or flashed input.
4. NIN, BVN and Exam PIN confirmations are sealed (encrypted; bound to the customer, service, plan, confirmed amount, a one-time token and the issue time), can be paid for exactly 10 minutes, fail closed and are sent no-store, private. Each flow has its own rate limiters (30 confirmations and 10 submissions per minute per customer; staff NIN/BVN search 20 per minute).
5. A NIN, BVN or Exam PIN purchase is successful only with the result its provider delivered (Exam PIN: at least one non-blank value); without it the purchase stays unclear (`result_missing`), is re-checked and is never refunded or failed over, and a purchase with a result is never refunded. Results are stored once, encrypted (the purchased NIN/BVN masked inside), and shown in full only on the purchasing customer's own result page under "Your result"; staff see only that a result exists and its field count. "No record found" is a definite failure with a refund unless a provider's official documentation and an explicit approval say otherwise.
6. Exam PIN: the existing `exam-pin` service, displayed as "Exam PIN" (slug unchanged, no migration); no customer recipient and no candidate-input workflow; fixed price, one plan is one PIN; no consent step; no PIN or serial search; a provider reference that repeats a delivered value is not stored.
7. D8: no retention or deletion of NIN/BVN numbers or purchase results is implemented. A retention policy is deferred and is not invented.
8. Smile Data stays blocked until a verified official provider specification exists and is approved; nothing about it is invented, it gets no new phase number, and it does not reuse NIN/BVN identity handling unless an approved official specification requires it.
9. No new staff permission: `purchases.view` and `purchases.manage` cover NIN, BVN and Exam PIN.
10. Maintenance mode blocks every new purchase, including NIN, BVN and Exam PIN.

## Approved decisions (Phase 12)
1. Level 1 only: a commission is paid only to the buyer's direct referrer.
2. Eligible referrers are active Subscriber, Vendor and Affiliate accounts. API Users and staff never earn, and an API User buyer generates no commission.
3. Qualifying services are Data, Airtime, NIN, BVN and Exam PIN. Smile Data stays blocked, and every other service earns nothing.
4. Only a successful purchase earns. Failed, pending, review and unknown purchases never earn.
5. D1: the commission base is the customer's final charge (`amount_kobo`), which includes the variable-plan fee when it is part of that amount. Provider cost and margin are never used. The formula and pricing are unchanged.
6. Rates and caps are set per qualifying service by staff with `referrals.manage`: a rate from 0% to 99.99% (0 to 9999 basis points) and a cap in kobo up to `pricing.max_amount_kobo`, with a reason of 10 to 500 characters. Commission = min(floor(amount_kobo × rate_bps ÷ 10,000), cap_kobo). A missing or zero rate or cap, or a commission below 1 kobo, creates no commission and no failed attempt. The rate and cap used are stored with the commission.
7. The commission is credited automatically to the referrer's Main Wallet, described "Referral commission". A commission failure never changes a successful purchase back to failed, and failed attempts are never retried.
8. Commissions are immutable, and their status is derived: Credited, Reversed or Cancelled. A reversal is full only and debits the full amount as "Commission reversal"; a cancellation moves no money. Both need `referrals.manage`, an active account, a one-time token and a reason of 10 to 500 characters. Each commission takes one action, ever.
9. Referral links are made only at signup and are permanent. `/register?ref=CODE` only pre-fills the code field. No cookie or browser storage is used, self-referral is refused, and there is no email or phone matching.
10. D2: referrers' names and emails in the admin commission list, search and detail require `customers.view`; `referrals.view` alone sees a safe customer reference (`Customer #id`). Referral Links keeps its approved visibility.
11. Permissions: `referrals.view` and `referrals.manage`, defined since Phase 3 and Super Admin only by default. Phase 12 adds no permission.
12. Ratel and Bangansuba are not providers (see Approved decisions (Phase 10), item 5).
